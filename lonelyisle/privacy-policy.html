Privacy Policy for LonelyIsle

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

1. Introduction

LonelyIsle is a spatial ambient audio experience designed to be private by default.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled when you use LonelyIsle.

2. Information We Collect

LonelyIsle does not require you to create a user account and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to us.

With your permission, the app may access your Photo Library locally to let you choose a custom portrait, save your Resident ID card, or export shareable images. Photos and generated images are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to us. The app operates primarily on your device. Information used for exploration, spatial audio, playback, and settings remains on your device and is not sent to us.

3. Data Storage and Use

App settings and preferences are stored locally on your device using iOS system storage.

Your Resident ID, registration date, and custom portrait image are processed and stored exclusively on your device. Information used for exploration, spatial audio, playback, and settings is processed and stored on your device and is not transmitted to us or shared with third parties.

If you enable Listening Reminder, reminder preferences and schedules are stored locally on your device and used only to deliver local notifications.

4. Third-Party Services

LonelyIsle does not use advertising networks, analytics platforms, or tracking technologies.

The app relies on Apple services for App Store distribution, in-app purchases, subscriptions, Family Sharing, and related functionality. Purchase sharing is handled by Apple according to Apple's policies. We do not receive family member information. Information processed by Apple is subject to Apple's own policies. We do not receive personal data from Apple beyond what is necessary for purchase verification and subscription status.

5. In-App Purchases

LonelyIsle offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions.

All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, collect, or store payment information.

Purchase history, billing information, and subscription management are handled by Apple.

6. Children's Privacy

LonelyIsle is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the "Last Updated" date will be revised accordingly.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or LonelyIsle, please contact:

support@louisbuilds.me

9. Your Rights

Because LonelyIsle does not collect personal information, we do not have personal data to access, modify, or delete.

Removing the app from your device deletes all locally stored data.
