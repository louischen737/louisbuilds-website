Frequently Asked Questions

LonelyIsle is an app where you explore islands and listen to spatial ambient sound that changes as you move through each environment.

You can explore in two different ways:

In Free mode, you move through the island at your own pace.
In Auto mode, the island gently carries you along a continuous path.

Your movement shapes how the sound unfolds.

A bird’s-eye view — wider, more atmospheric sound.
A zoomed-in view — closer detail, with clearer local sounds.
