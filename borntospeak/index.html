Background · English Lab

Born To Speak began in Shanghai in spring 2024 as a spoken-English community, with the line “Born To Speak, Make Your Voice Heard” and shared values of respect, inclusiveness, and growth.

English Lab is this site: the four modules above bring together games, workplace scripts, discussion prompts, and companion apps—open materials you can browse and use at your own pace.
