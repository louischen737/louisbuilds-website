GLOB-02 · FlyTech

Global smartphone product development

全球智能手机产品开发：将市场调研结论落地为软硬件规格，按区域划分旗舰与中端产品策略。

From research insights to hardware/software choices: align specs with regional expectations, then split a flagship path vs a value-tier path.

重点练习产品会议高频搭配：align with、incorporate、tailor to 等专业表达。

参会角色 — Lily Wang (市场总监) · Tom Zhang (产品研发经理) · David Liu (市场研究员)

调研转化→产品规格 区域定制化 产品路线与负责人
会议形式
PD workshop · ~50 min
产品研发研讨会 · 约 50 分钟
会议目标
Two-model strategy + milestones
双机型策略+项目里程碑
练习重点
Align with, incorporate, tailor to
需求对齐、调研融入、区域定制

1. Opening & agenda

delve into 表示深入探讨；会议议程先回顾调研，再研讨产品规格。

Lily

Good morning, everyone. I’m delighted to have you here for this crucial meeting. I’m Lily Wang, marketing director. With me are Tom Zhang, product development manager, and David Liu, market research analyst. Let’s get started.

Lily

The purpose of this meeting is to delve into our new smartphone for overseas markets—we need the product to align with what global customers actually want. Here’s the agenda: research recap, specs and features, then target-market implications. Let’s begin with the market research.

2. Insights & product response

先由研究员分享核心洞察，再由研发说明如何融入产品；针对不同区域制定旗舰/中端机型策略。

Lily

David—can you share the key insights from the market research?

David

Sure. North America wants 5G, multi-lens cameras, long battery life, plus a seamless UX and regular software updates. Europe emphasizes eco-friendly materials, energy-efficient devices, and privacy and security. Southeast Asia prioritizes affordability, solid basics, and dual-SIM support.

Lily

That’s very informative. Tom—how do we incorporate these findings into product development?

Tom

For North America, we should aim for a flagship—latest 5G chipset, strong camera stack, large battery, polished UI with frequent updates. For Europe, recycled materials, tighter power management. For Southeast Asia, a mid-range SKU with cost-effective BoM but reliable performance.

「BoM」= Bill of Materials，即产品物料清单与成本结构。

Lily

That sounds like a solid plan. Now, let’s move on to product specifications and features. Tom?

Tom

We should target at least Full HD display, a processor that handles multitasking, storage tiers from 64GB to 256GB to cater to different users, and fast charging. David suggested better zoom, night mode, and AI-enhanced photography. I’d add differentiated software—e.g. a personalized assistant or built-in security suite—plus a sleek, durable industrial design.

3. Target markets & tailoring

elaborate on 表示详细说明；go-to-market bundles 指各区域市场的产品打包方案。

Lily

Moving on to the target market. David—can you elaborate on the specific needs in each region?

David

North America: tech-savvy buyers, premium features, brand reputation, strong after-sales service. Europe: sustainability and ethically produced devices. Southeast Asia: price-sensitive but expects value for money—localization and language support matter.

Tom

So we tailor go-to-market bundles: NA/EU high-end with premium support; SEA optimize cost structure and ship localized software.

Lily

That makes sense.

4. Decisions & close

基于会议讨论分配机型研发负责人；reconvene 表示召开后续会议。

Lily

Based on our discussion: Tom, you’ll lead two SKUs—a flagship for North America and Europe, and a mid-range for Southeast Asia. David, you’ll keep monitoring the market and flag shifts. To sum up, we’ve aligned requirements and how we tailor by region. Let’s execute—Tom, I want a detailed roadmap and specs in the next few weeks; David, bi-weekly market updates. We’ll reconvene in three weeks. Thank you all—that’s it for today.

Tom

Got it. I’ll keep development on track.

David

Sure—I’ll keep a close eye on the market.
