Speaking lab

Low-stakes games, high-output English

In the BTS community, board games work as social simulators—these picks force you past translation and build real-time English reflexes.

1

Taboo

Circumlocution

👪 4–10 ⌛ ~20m

How it works

Describe the target word without using it or five forbidden keywords—forces paraphrasing under pressure.

Why it helps

Builds “explain simply” fluency when you forget the exact word.

2

Dixit

Abstract description

👪 3–6 ⌛ ~30m

Surreal art cards + cryptic one-line clues—others match meanings. Rewards vivid, metaphor-rich English.

Why it helps

Stretches adjectives, tone, and mood beyond textbook nouns.

3

Codenames

Semantic web

👪 4–8 ⌛ ~30m

Spymasters give one-word clues linking multiple words—teammates guess carefully and avoid the assassin.

Why it helps

Trains grouping, nuance, and one-word summarization.

4

Once Upon a Time

Narrative flow

👪 2–6 ⌛ ~45m

Co-create a story with prompt cards; interrupt and steal the narrative—race to play your ending.

Why it helps

Great for connectors, pacing, and holding the floor in English.

5

Word on the Street

Rapid recall

👪 2–8 ⌛ ~20m

Tug-of-war for letters: brainstorm category words and pull letters toward your side before time runs out.

Why it helps

Competition + timer = faster word retrieval and retention.
