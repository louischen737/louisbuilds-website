The “IPA formatting” headache

Every English teacher knows the struggle: copying IPA symbols from an online dictionary, only to have the font break, the alignment shift, or the formatting disappear when pasted into Word. It’s a time-consuming barrier to creating high-quality teaching materials.

Beyond simple translation

Phonetic Formatter was built to solve the presentation problem. It doesn’t just give you the IPA; it creates a structured, word-aligned layout.

  • Vertical alignment: Each word is paired with its phonetic form in a clear, consistent layout.
  • Sentence-level processing: Process entire paragraphs while keeping a natural reading flow.

Built for the classroom (and the university)

Whether you are a Gymnasium teacher in Germany preparing for a phonetics unit or a linguistics student in the US, accuracy and exportability are key.

  • Export with ease: One-tap export to DOCX or PDF means your worksheets are ready for print in seconds, with reliable IPA rendering in the exported file.
  • Standardized accuracy: Powered by the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, giving a consistent American English (General American) baseline. See also why we build offline-first.

The offline advantage in education

School Wi-Fi can be unreliable, and student privacy is paramount. By working 100% offline, Phonetic Formatter lets you prepare lessons anywhere—from a commute to a classroom—without compromising data security.
