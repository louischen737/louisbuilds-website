A simple way to take a break from your phone

TapPause is a minimal mindfulness app designed for people who want to pause without pressure. It works as a distraction-free pause timer — no tracking, no streaks, no engagement loops.

Unlike many meditation apps, TapPause does not collect data or measure performance. It simply creates a quiet digital space where you can slow down during a busy day.

Whether you need a short mindful break at work or a small digital detox moment at night, TapPause helps you step out of constant motion — even briefly.
