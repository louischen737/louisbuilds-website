Why Is Focus Difficult with ADHD?

ADHD makes it harder to manage attention, especially when tasks feel overwhelming or unclear. Distractions, lack of structure, and mental fatigue can make it difficult to start and stay focused.

What Helps Improve Focus?

  • Short, structured work sessions
  • Clear start and end points
  • Reducing visual and mental clutter
  • Gentle reminders instead of pressure

Tips for Staying Focused with ADHD

  • Remove distractions before starting
  • Use timers instead of relying on willpower
