Why Use a Pomodoro Timer?

A Pomodoro timer helps you stay focused by breaking work into structured sessions with short breaks. This technique is widely used for deep work and study sessions, making it easier to build consistent focus habits, improve productivity, and reduce mental fatigue over time.
