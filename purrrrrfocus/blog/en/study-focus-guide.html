3 Science-Backed Study Hacks

1. The Pomodoro Rhythm (25/5 Rule)

Use a timer like PurrrrrFocus to commit to just 25 minutes of deep work. Knowing there is an end in sight reduces the "pain of starting" that causes procrastination. If you want to compare Pomodoro apps: The 3 Best Pomodoro Timer Apps for Deep Work (2026).

2. Active Recall & Spaced Repetition

Don't just re-read notes. Close the book and test yourself. Pair these intense mental sprints with a calm focus timer to keep your momentum.

3. Digital Detox Environment

Your phone is a "dopamine slot machine." Turn on Elite Focus Mode in your app to discourage phone usage while you study.
