Step1st

iOS · Health & Fitness · Productivity · v1.0

A movement-driven iOS app blocker that links screen time to physical activity—using step goals before you scroll.

Learn More →

Unlock your apps, one step at a time.

Designed for desk-bound professionals and chronic doom-scrollers, Step1st uses physical step goals to break sedentary loops and enforce a "Move-to-Unlock" daily ritual. Step1st solves the modern dilemma of digital addiction coupled with physical inactivity. By linking screen time to movement, it ensures you hit your activity targets before surrendering to distractions. It’s not just an app locker; it’s a self-discipline engine for those who want to reclaim their health by making movement the prerequisite for entertainment.

  • Integrity-Based Locking: Uses Apple’s Screen Time API to keep distracting apps inaccessible until your steps are logged—eliminating the "Ignore Limit" loophole common in basic timers.
  • Frictionless Tracking: Leverages native motion co-processors for high-precision step counting with zero location tracking, protecting your privacy and your battery.
  • HealthKit Empowerment: Seamlessly syncs with Apple Health to provide a "Read-Only" experience, keeping your data local and under your total control.
  • Native Efficiency: Designed with a "Zero-Impact" philosophy—running ultra-lightweight in the background to ensure your iPhone stays fast and responsive.

TransMov

macOS · Graphics & Design · Productivity · v2.0

A local-first macOS utility that extracts transparent animated assets from video—for UI designers and web developers.

Learn More →

A specialized layer for UI asset extraction.

TransMov is a local-first macOS utility for UI designers and web developers who need transparent animated assets from video—without cloud uploads. It sits between your source footage and production code, converting clips into transparent APNG or WebP locally on your Mac so sensitive brand assets stay on-device.

Understand different use cases and tradeoffs:

Local vs Cloud →
  • UI-Ready Transparency: Specialized in generating 8-bit alpha-blended assets to prevent the jagged edges common in traditional GIFs.
  • Local-Only Processing: Uses on-device AI via Apple Vision. Your source video stays 100% private, processed entirely on your machine.
  • Focused on Web Formats: Prioritizes modern web-native formats like APNG and WebP over cinematic video containers.
  • Fit for UI Workflows: Ideal for onboarding animations and product demos where privacy and web compatibility matter more than cinematic precision.
