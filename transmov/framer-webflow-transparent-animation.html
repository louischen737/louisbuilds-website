Cross-Browser Implementation Guide

Transparent web animations need different strategies depending on your platform and compatibility goals. Pick the workflow that fits your stack:

Method 1: Maximum Compatibility (Recommended)

The safest choice for Webflow HTML Embeds and any custom code block. A single high-fidelity APNG plays with true alpha transparency across all major browsers, fully avoiding animated WebP issues on legacy iOS Safari (14–15).

<!-- Paste inside Webflow HTML Embed or any custom HTML block -->
<img src="your-animation.png" alt="TransMov Transparent Asset" style="width:100%; height:auto;">

Method 2: Performance Optimization (Advanced)

Serve lightweight WebP to browsers that support it, with APNG as the fallback when WebP is unavailable. Best when your audience is primarily on desktop or Safari 16+. Note: Safari 14–15 may still select WebP and freeze on the first frame — use Method 1 for those users.

<!-- Layered optimization snippet -->
<picture>
    <source srcset="your-animation.webp" type="image/webp">
    <img src="your-animation.png" alt="TransMov Transparent Asset" style="width:100%; height:auto;">
</picture>

Method 3: For Framer (Zero-Code)

Drag the APNG (.png) or WebP exported from TransMov directly onto your Framer canvas — no embed code required. For the most reliable transparent playback on all mobile breakpoints, prefer APNG; WebP works well when your audience is on Safari 16+ and other modern browsers.
