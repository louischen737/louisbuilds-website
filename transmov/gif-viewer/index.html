Is my GIF uploaded to a server?

No. Your GIF stays on your device. TransMov GIF Viewer processes files directly in your browser, so your animations are never uploaded or stored on a server.

You can preview the animation and check details including file name, file size, dimensions, frame count, and duration. You can also switch between checkerboard, black, and white backgrounds to inspect transparency.

Can GIFs support transparency?

GIF supports basic transparency, but it cannot display full alpha transparency like APNG or WebP. For smoother edges and more advanced transparency, compare GIF with newer animation formats in our GIF vs APNG vs WebP guide.
