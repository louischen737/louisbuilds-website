Core Capabilities

What you can do with this English-to-IPA transcription tool.

Phonetic Formatter converts real English text into clear, structured IPA phonetic transcription designed for study, teaching, and linguistic analysis. Instead of simple word lookups, the app processes complete sentences and paragraphs while preserving readable formatting.

Sentence-Level Transcription

Convert full English sentences and paragraphs into structured IPA transcription while preserving natural sentence boundaries.

The formatter processes real-world text including abbreviations, numbers, percentages, domain names, and punctuation, so entire passages can be transcribed without manual cleanup.

This makes it suitable for classroom materials, pronunciation study, and linguistic analysis where complete text passages are required.

Word‑by‑Word Alignment (Pro)

Display each word together with its corresponding IPA pronunciation in a structured alignment format.

This layout makes it easy to examine pronunciation at the individual word level while still preserving the full sentence context.

It is especially useful for teaching, worksheet preparation, pronunciation analysis, and classroom projection, where learners need to connect written words with their phonetic representation.

Clean, Readable Formatting

Outputs are formatted into clear transcription blocks that pair original text with IPA lines in a consistent, readable structure.

Stress marks, spacing, and punctuation are preserved so the transcription remains easy to read even for long passages or academic materials.

The result is designed to be practical for study notes, teaching resources, and linguistic documentation.

Fully Offline & Private

All transcription is performed locally on your device, with no cloud processing required.

Your text never leaves the device, which ensures privacy, reliability, and instant response time.

This makes the tool suitable for classroom environments, research work, and situations where internet access or data sharing is restricted.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an entire English paragraph to IPA?

Open Phonetic Formatter, paste your paragraph into the text field, and tap Convert. The app transcribes the whole passage at once using the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. You can choose sentence-level output or, with Pro, word-by-word alignment. Results can be copied or exported to TXT, DOCX, or PDF.

Is there an offline IPA transcriber for iOS?

Yes. Phonetic Formatter runs fully offline on your iPhone or iPad. All phonetic conversion happens on device—no internet required and no data is sent to servers. It works anywhere: in the classroom, on the go, or in areas with poor connectivity.
