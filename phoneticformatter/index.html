What Is IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet)?

The International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) is a system for representing the sounds of spoken language. It shows how words are actually pronounced, rather than how they are spelled.

Example: through /θruː/  ·  thought /θɔːt/

Learn more about IPA transcription

Each IPA symbol corresponds to a specific sound, making pronunciation clear and consistent across languages.

An English to IPA converter transforms written text into phonetic transcription, helping learners, teachers, and linguists study pronunciation more effectively.

Detailed IPA Transcription Capabilities

This English to IPA converter is designed to process real-world text and produce accurate, structured phonetic transcription using the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA).

Sentence-Level IPA Transcription

The system converts complete English sentences and paragraphs into IPA phonetic transcription while preserving punctuation, spacing, and natural sentence boundaries.

Unlike basic phonetic converters that rely on isolated dictionary lookups, it processes continuous text as a whole, maintaining readability and structural coherence.

It supports real-world input such as abbreviations, numbers, percentages, domain names, and mixed punctuation, allowing full passages to be transcribed without manual preprocessing.

This makes it suitable for pronunciation practice, classroom materials, and linguistic analysis where full-text transcription is required.

Word-by-Word IPA Alignment

Each word in the original text is aligned with its corresponding IPA transcription, creating a clear mapping between spelling and pronunciation.

This alignment format allows users to examine pronunciation at the individual word level while still preserving full sentence context.

The structured layout is particularly useful for teaching, worksheet preparation, and pronunciation training, where learners need to connect written forms with phonetic representation.

It also supports detailed analysis of stress patterns, syllable structure, and phonetic variation across words in a sentence.

Readable and Structured IPA Formatting

Transcription output is formatted into clean, consistent blocks that pair original text with IPA lines, making the result easy to read and use.

Stress marks, spacing, and punctuation are preserved to maintain clarity, even when processing longer passages or complex text.

The formatting is designed for practical use in study notes, teaching materials, and linguistic documentation, rather than raw or unstructured output.

This ensures that IPA transcription remains accessible not only to linguists but also to learners and educators.

Offline IPA Transcription and Privacy

All transcription is performed locally on your device, with no cloud processing or external API calls required.

This guarantees that your text remains private and is never transmitted or stored externally.

Offline processing also ensures consistent performance, instant response time, and reliability in environments with limited or no internet access.

It is particularly suitable for classrooms, research settings, and professional workflows where data privacy and independence from online services are important.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an entire English paragraph to IPA?

Open Phonetic Formatter, paste your paragraph into the text field, and tap Convert. The app transcribes the whole passage at once using the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. You can choose sentence-level output or, with Pro, word-by-word alignment. Results can be copied or exported to TXT, DOCX, or PDF.

Is there an offline IPA transcriber for iOS?

Yes. Phonetic Formatter runs fully offline on your iPhone or iPad. All phonetic conversion happens on device—no internet required and no data is sent to servers. It works anywhere: in the classroom, on the go, or in areas with poor connectivity.

Can I use this app on my iPad? Do I need to pay again?

Absolutely! The app is fully optimized for iPad. If you have already purchased the Pro version on your iPhone, simply log in with the same Apple ID on your iPad and use "Restore Purchase" to activate Pro features at no extra cost.

Note: To protect your privacy, the app does not store any transcription history. All processing is done instantly and locally.

Does this converter use the CMU Pronunciation Dictionary?

Yes, our tool is powered by the CMU Pronunciation Dictionary, widely considered a gold standard in North American English NLP. However, we've enhanced it with custom logic for better sentence-level transcription and stress marking.
