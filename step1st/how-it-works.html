How Step1st Works

Lock apps until you reach your daily step goal. Walk first, unlock later.

1

Select Apps

Pick the apps you want to pause for the day — social media, games, or anything distracting.

2

Set Daily Step Goal

Choose a realistic step goal based on your activity level. Step1st will track your progress automatically.

3

Walk to Unlock

Walk until your daily step goal is met, then your apps unlock for the rest of the day.
