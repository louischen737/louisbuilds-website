A smarter way to reduce screen time

Step1st is a step goal productivity app designed to reduce phone distractions by connecting screen time with real-world movement.

Instead of blocking apps with timers, Step1st lets your daily walking goal decide when apps unlock. Social media, games, and other distracting apps stay paused until you reach your step milestone.

This simple system builds a healthier habit: move first, scroll later.
