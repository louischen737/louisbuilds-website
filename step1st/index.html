Common Questions

What is Step1st?
Step1st is an iPhone app blocker and digital wellness app that puts distracting apps behind a daily step goal. You choose the apps, set a movement goal, and unlock access after walking enough steps.
How is Step1st different from Apple Screen Time?
Apple Screen Time is mainly timer-based and can often be bypassed with a tap. Step1st adds a movement-based barrier: selected apps stay blocked until your step goal is reached, making the pause physical instead of purely willpower-based. Read why Screen Time limits are easy to ignore.
Can Step1st block apps on iPhone?
Yes. Step1st works as an app blocker using Apple’s Screen Time API. You can select apps to lock, and they will only unlock after you reach your daily step goal. Unlike traditional app blockers, there’s no easy override—your movement becomes the key. Learn how the step-based app blocker works.
How can I try Step1st right now?
Download Step1st free on the App Store for iPhone (iOS 16 or later). Pro features are available via in-app subscription.
Who is Step1st for?
Step1st is for people who open social apps, short video apps, or other distracting apps on autopilot and want a healthier interruption. It can also help families who want a simple way to connect screen time with daily movement, or anyone trying to stop doomscrolling without relying on willpower.
Can I change my step goal?
Yes. Your step goal is adjustable. Step1st is designed to create helpful friction, not trap you behind a rigid rule that does not fit real life.
Does Step1st use GPS or location tracking?
No. Step1st does not require GPS or location tracking. Steps are handled through iPhone motion data and optional Apple Health integration.
Does it drain my battery?
Not at all. We built Step1st using the latest Apple native technology, designed specifically to be ultra-efficient. It works quietly in the background without noticeably affecting your battery life.
Is my data private?
Absolutely. Your movement data and app rules are processed locally. While we analyze general usage patterns to improve the experience, this data is completely anonymized and is never linked to your personal identity. Your health records remain read-only and under your total control. Learn how we protect your privacy.

Built on Privacy. Focused on You.

Step1st is designed to respect your boundaries. We use industry-standard encryption and Apple’s native frameworks to ensure your journey remains private and secure.

Native App Control

We utilize Apple’s official Family Controls to manage app limits. This system-level integration means Step1st never has access to your personal content, messages, or browsing history.

Anonymous Movement

Your steps are processed locally on your device via motion sensors. We track activity trends to help you improve, but we never link this data to your real-world identity or location.

Secure Integration

Our HealthKit sync is strictly read-only and remains under your total control. Your health records are never modified, never shared with third parties, and never sold.
