Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Step1st ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Step1st mobile application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App.

By using Step1st, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

Step1st is designed to help users stay focused by temporarily locking selected apps until a daily step goal is reached.

We believe privacy is fundamental. Step1st is built with the following principles:

  • Your personal data stays on your device whenever possible
  • We collect minimal information
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data
  • We do not require user accounts

Most data used by Step1st is processed locally on your device.

2. Information We Access

Step1st may request access to certain system permissions to provide its features.

Motion & Fitness Data

Step1st may request permission to access Motion & Fitness activity to count your steps.

This allows the app to:

  • track your daily step progress
  • determine when your daily step goal has been reached
  • unlock apps once the goal is met

This data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

Health App (HealthKit)

Step1st may optionally request permission to read step count data from the Apple Health app.

If granted, Step1st may read:

  • step count data

This allows Step1st to:

  • display your walking history
  • calculate trends and statistics
  • show longer-term step insights

Step1st:

  • only reads step count data
  • does not write or modify any Health data
  • does not store Health data on external servers

All HealthKit data usage complies with Apple's HealthKit guidelines.

Screen Time Access

Step1st may request permission to access Screen Time APIs in order to temporarily restrict selected apps.

This permission allows the app to:

  • pause selected apps until your daily step goal is reached
  • automatically unlock apps once the goal is completed

Step1st does not collect or transmit Screen Time activity data outside your device.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

Step1st does not collect the following information:

  • personal identity information (name, email, phone number)
  • location data
  • contact lists
  • photos or files
  • browsing history
  • messages
  • health records other than step count (if HealthKit is enabled)

Step1st also does not create user accounts.

4. Data Storage

All data used by Step1st is stored locally on your device, including:

  • selected apps to lock
  • daily step goals
  • step progress
  • app settings

If you delete the app, this data will be removed.

5. Analytics

Step1st may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand general app usage and improve the product.

Analytics data may include:

  • app version
  • device type
  • basic interaction events (such as opening certain screens)

This data:

  • does not identify individual users
  • does not include health data
  • does not include Screen Time data

6. In-App Purchases

Step1st offers optional Pro subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

When you make a purchase:

  • the transaction is processed by Apple
  • Step1st does not receive your payment information

Apple may provide limited purchase status information to verify your subscription.

For details, please review Apple's privacy policy.

7. Third-Party Services

Step1st relies on the following Apple services:

  • Apple HealthKit
  • Motion & Fitness framework
  • Screen Time APIs
  • App Store in-app purchases

These services are governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

8. Children's Privacy

Step1st is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When updates are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.

Continued use of the App after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
